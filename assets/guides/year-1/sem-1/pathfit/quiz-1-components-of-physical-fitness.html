STUDY FIRST
5+ Examples Per Component

Concrete exercises and scenarios for each component — many drawn from basketball since that's your reference sport, plus other team and individual activities for variety. Use these to build your own scenario answers.

Health-Related (5)
Cardiorespiratory Endurance
Heart/lungs/blood vessels sustaining activity over time
  • Full-court basketball press defense for an entire quarter
  • Running a 5K or long-distance jog
  • Swimming continuous laps
  • Playing a full 90-minute soccer match
  • Cycling for 30+ minutes straight
Muscular Strength
Max force in a single effort
  • Boxing out a bigger opponent for one rebound
  • A one-rep max bench press or deadlift
  • A single max-effort pull-up
  • A shot-put throw
  • Pushing a stalled car or heavy sled once
Muscular Endurance
Repeated contractions over time without tiring
  • 100 defensive slide-drills in a row at basketball practice
  • Doing 50 continuous push-ups
  • Paddling through an entire dragon boat race
  • Holding a plank for several minutes
  • Repeated jump-shot reps (100+) in shooting practice
Flexibility
Full range of motion at a joint
  • A point guard's deep lunge stretch before driving to the basket
  • A goalkeeper's split-stretch dive to save a shot
  • A gymnast performing a full split
  • Yoga poses like downward dog or cobra
  • A swimmer's shoulder rotation on freestyle stroke
Body Composition
Fat mass vs. lean mass ratio
  • Comparing skinfold measurements before/after a training block
  • A wrestler cutting weight for his weight class
  • Two players, same height/weight, different muscle-to-fat ratio
  • Tracking BMI change over a semester
  • A bodybuilder working on muscle definition pre-competition
Skill-Related (6)
Power
Max force in minimum time (strength + speed)
  • An explosive dunk over a defender
  • A volleyball player's spike
  • A sprinter's start out of the blocks
  • A boxer's power punch
  • A high jumper's takeoff
Speed
Covering distance in the shortest time
  • Sprinting alone down the court on a fast break after a steal
  • A 100m dash
  • A soccer winger racing past a defender to a through ball
  • Stealing a base in baseball
  • Chasing down a loose ball before an opponent
Agility
Changing direction quickly, with control
  • A crossover dribble to blow past a defender
  • Weaving through cones in a soccer drill
  • A boxer's footwork dodging punches
  • A badminton player lunging for a drop shot then recovering
  • A tennis player scrambling back after a wide shot
Coordination
Senses + body parts working smoothly together
  • Dribbling while watching the court to set up a pass
  • Executing a layup — footwork, hand placement, and timing at once
  • Hitting a baseball with a bat (hand-eye)
  • Jump rope with matched foot/hand timing
  • Juggling
Reaction Time
Responding quickly to a stimulus
  • A defender's hand deflecting a pass the instant it's thrown
  • A goalkeeper diving to block a penalty kick
  • A sprinter reacting to the starting gun
  • A boxer slipping a punch at the last moment
  • A badminton player returning a smash
Balance
Keeping center of gravity over base of support
  • Landing a contested jump shot and absorbing a bump without falling
  • A gymnast on the balance beam
  • A surfer riding a wave
  • Holding a yoga tree pose
  • A soccer player staying upright after a tackle attempt
