2.5 Types of Computers (slides 28–35)

TypeSource description
Microcomputer (s28)A personal computer; designed to meet the computer needs of an individual. Provides access to a wide variety of computing applications, such as word processing, photo editing, e-mail, and internet.
Desktop Microcomputer (s29)A microcomputer that fits on a desk and runs on power from an electrical wall outlet. The CPU can be housed in either a vertical or a horizontal case. Has separate components (keyboard, mouse, etc.) that are each plugged into the computer.
Laptop Computer (s30)A portable, compact computer that can run on an electrical wall outlet or a battery unit. All components (keyboard, mouse, etc.) are in one compact unit. Usually more expensive than a comparable desktop. Sometimes called a Notebook.

Server (s34)Purpose is to "serve." A computer that has the purpose of supplying its users with data; usually through the use of a LAN (local area network).

Typically used as an appointment book, address book, calculator, and notepad. Can be synchronized with a personal microcomputer as a backup.
